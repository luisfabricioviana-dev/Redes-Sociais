## Candidate mechanisms

### `EVERGREEN_CATALOG_ASSET_STRATEGY_v1`
Treat high-investment videos as durable catalog assets, not disposable posts. Evaluate launch performance plus long-tail discovery and continued audience value.

### `CATALOG_SESSION_PROPAGATION_v1`
`VIDEO PERFORMANCE ≠ ISOLATED VIDEO PERFORMANCE`. Measure whether an entry video propagates viewers into adjacent catalog assets and extends channel sessions.

### `PRODUCTION_LEVERAGE_GATE_v1`
Before increasing production complexity, require material improvement in comprehension, retention, credibility, evidentiary clarity, truth, or emotional impact. Prioritize idea, angle, hook, packaging and script before expensive polish.

### `PACKAGING_PROMISE_FULFILLMENT_GATE_v1`
`TITLE + THUMBNAIL → IMPLIED PROMISE → OPENING → STORY → PAYOFF`. High CTR with weak promise fulfillment is failed packaging.

### `VIEWER_BEHAVIOR_FIRST_v1`
Do not canonize simplified claims about “the algorithm”. Optimize observable viewer decisions such as click, continue, abandon, watch another asset, and return.

### `DELAYED_DISCOVERY_AWARENESS_v1`
`EARLY UNDERPERFORMANCE ≠ PERMANENT FAILURE`. Distinguish launch weakness, packaging failure, content-market mismatch, delayed discovery, evergreen long-tail and external amplification when evidence permits.

### `CREATIVE_COMPLEXITY_DEBT_v1`
Production complexity can reduce iteration frequency and learning velocity while increasing stakes per asset. Higher production value must not destroy iteration velocity without justified return.

### `EXPERIMENTATION_LATENCY_v1`
Measure time from hypothesis → implementation → result → next iteration. Premium workflows should retain lower-cost/high-learning test paths where possible.

### `PRODUCTION_SYSTEM_REVIEW_v1`
Periodically inspect the production system itself: workflow map → stage durations → rework → bottlenecks → human/tool constraints → automation/delegation opportunities → redesign.
